# OJAS — 2-Week Social Media Content Calendar

Ready-to-post captions, hashtags, and short-form video scripts for Instagram and TikTok. Written for accounts you create and own yourself — nothing here assumes a live account already exists.

Handle suggestion: **@ojas.fit** (check availability before registering).

Posting cadence: 1 Instagram post/Reel + 1 TikTok per day, 5 days/week.

---

## Week 1

### Day 1 — Launch Teaser (Reel / TikTok)
**Format:** 15-sec vertical video, quick cuts.
**Script:** Open on a dark screen with the OJAS flame logo animating in. Cut to 3 quick clips: someone cooking a Thai curry, someone mid-yoga pose, someone logging a workout on phone. Text overlay: "Train. Nourish. Rise." Final frame: "OJAS — coming to your feed."
**Caption:** "Something's training. 🔥 Follow for the launch. #OJASFit #TrainNourishRise #FitnessLaunch"
**Hashtags:** #OJASFit #FitnessMotivation #GlobalFitness #NutritionApp #TrainNourishRise

### Day 2 — Meet the Meals (Carousel)
**Format:** 6-slide carousel, one slide per country (India, Italy, Mexico, Japan, Thailand, Mediterranean).
**Caption:** "One plan. Every country's comfort food. Swipe to see today's menu around the world. 🌍 Which one are you making tonight? #OJASFit #WorldOnAPlate"
**Hashtags:** #HealthyRecipes #MealPrep #GlobalCuisine #OJASFit #NutritionCoach

### Day 3 — Exercise Library Spotlight (Reel / TikTok)
**Format:** 20-sec demo, split-screen Beginner vs. Advanced variation of the same move (e.g. Push-ups).
**Script:** Text overlay "Same move. Your level." Left side: beginner push-up form. Right side: advanced tempo push-up. End card: "24 exercises. Every level. Link in bio."
**Caption:** "Strength, cardio, yoga, core, flexibility, HIIT — pick your level, not just your workout. #OJASFit #ExerciseLibrary"
**Hashtags:** #WorkoutMotivation #HomeWorkout #StrengthTraining #OJASFit #FitTok

### Day 4 — Founder-Style Voiceover (Reel / TikTok)
**Format:** Talking-head or voiceover over app screen recording.
**Script:** "Every fitness app tells you what to eat. Ours actually asks where you are first." Screen recording of switching the country filter on the Meals tab. "That's OJAS." End card with logo.
**Caption:** "Why we built the Meals tab this way. #OJASFit #BehindTheApp"
**Hashtags:** #FitnessApp #NutritionTech #OJASFit #StartupStory

### Day 5 — Member Review Repost (Static / Carousel)
**Format:** Styled quote card using a review from the in-app Reviews page (with the member's first name + city, as shown in-app).
**Caption:** "This is why we build the way we do. 💬 Real feedback from real members. #OJASFit #MemberLove"
**Hashtags:** #Testimonial #FitnessCommunity #OJASFit #RealResults

## Week 2

### Day 6 — Day-in-the-Life (Reel / TikTok)
**Format:** 30-sec montage, morning-to-night.
**Script:** 6am alarm → OJAS daily plan notification → yoga flow → breakfast (Masala Oats) → workday b-roll → evening HIIT circuit → dinner (Grilled Fish, Mediterranean) → streak counter ticking up on phone.
**Caption:** "A full day, one plan. What does your OJAS day look like? #OJASFit #DayInMyLife"
**Hashtags:** #FitnessRoutine #DailyPlan #OJASFit #WellnessJourney

### Day 7 — Myth-Bust (Talking-head)
**Format:** 25-sec direct-to-camera.
**Script:** "Myth: you need to eat bland food to hit your macros. Truth: [show Kung Pao Chicken recipe card] this is 380 calories and full of flavor." End card: "Meals tab, all 8 countries."
**Caption:** "You don't have to choose between flavor and your goals. #OJASFit #NutritionMyths"
**Hashtags:** #NutritionFacts #HealthyEating #OJASFit #MythBusting

### Day 8 — Plan Comparison (Carousel)
**Format:** 4-slide carousel: Basic vs. Pro vs. Premium feature breakdown, final slide with the discount code callout.
**Caption:** "Which plan is your speed? Basic, Pro, or Premium — all start under ₹300/month. Use code FIT10 this week. #OJASFit #FitnessPlans"
**Hashtags:** #FitnessSubscription #OJASFit #WellnessPlan #DiscountCode

### Day 9 — Exercise Demo Series: Core (Reel / TikTok)
**Format:** 4-move core circuit, quick-cut demo (Plank, Russian Twists, Bicycle Crunches, Hanging Leg Raises).
**Script:** Each move gets 3 seconds of clean demo footage with on-screen name + rep count, upbeat transition sound between each.
**Caption:** "10-minute core circuit, straight from the OJAS Exercise Library. Save this for leg day's rest day. #OJASFit #CoreWorkout"
**Hashtags:** #AbWorkout #CoreStrength #OJASFit #FitnessChallenge

### Day 10 — UGC Prompt / Streak Challenge Launch (Static + Reel)
**Format:** Announcement graphic + 15-sec hype reel.
**Script:** Fast cuts of streak counters (7, 14, 30 days) ticking up with celebratory sound. Text: "7-Day Reset Challenge starts Monday. Tag @ojas.fit to be featured."
**Caption:** "Starting Monday: the OJAS 7-Day Reset. Log a workout + a meal every day for 7 days, tag us, and get featured on our page. Who's in? #OJASFit #7DayReset"
