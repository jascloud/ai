# OJAS Marketing Campaign Concepts

Four campaign concepts for OJAS, positioned as a global fitness + nutrition platform with Indian roots. Written as strategy/creative concepts for you to adapt — none of this references or copies any other company's actual campaigns, creative, or trademarks.

---

## 1. Launch Campaign — "Train Anywhere"

**Objective:** Drive new sign-ups for the Basic/Pro/Premium plans in the first 90 days post-launch.

**Audience:** Urban professionals aged 22–40 in India and the diaspora, plus general global fitness-app shoppers, who've tried at least one other fitness app and dropped off.

**Channels:** Instagram/TikTok paid + organic, influencer seeding (micro-creators, 10K–100K followers, fitness/nutrition niche), app store optimization, referral landing page.

**Core Message:** "Your body doesn't care what timezone you're in." — one plan that flexes across geography, cuisine, and schedule instead of forcing a single template on everyone.

**Sample Ad Copy:**
- *Headline:* "Dal one day. Ramen the next. Same plan."
  *Body:* "OJAS builds your day around real food from wherever you are — 8 cuisines, one plan. Start free for 7 days."
- *Headline:* "Skip the app graveyard."
  *Body:* "You've downloaded three fitness apps this year. OJAS is the one built to survive week three. Try Basic for ₹99."

**Content Ideas:** "Day in the life" reels across 3 cities (Mumbai, Milan, Bangkok) using the same OJAS plan; before/after onboarding screen recordings; founder-style narrated walkthrough of the daily plan generator.

**KPIs:** Cost per install, trial-to-paid conversion rate (target 18–22%), 7-day retention.

## 2. Product Campaign — "Real Food, Real World"

**Objective:** Differentiate OJAS's meal library (33 recipes, 8 countries) against single-cuisine diet apps; drive Meals-tab engagement and Pro-tier upgrades ("all cuisines unlocked").

**Audience:** Existing free/Basic users who've gone quiet on meal logging; nutrition-curious users comparing apps.

**Channels:** In-app upgrade prompts, Instagram carousels, TikTok recipe-in-60-seconds series, email nurture sequence.

**Core Message:** "One subscription. Every country's comfort food, still on-plan."

**Sample Ad Copy:**
- *Headline:* "Your dinner shouldn't feel like homework."
  *Body:* "Switch between Thai green curry, Greek chickpea salad, and dal tadka — same calorie target, zero spreadsheet."
- *Carousel hook:* "8 countries. 1 plan. Swipe to see today's menu."

**Content Ideas:** Weekly "Around the World in a Meal Plan" carousel featuring one country's full day of meals; short recipe-prep reels tied directly to in-app recipes; user-submitted plate photos (with consent) reposted as a recurring feature slot.

**KPIs:** Meals-tab weekly active users, Pro upgrade rate attributed to campaign, recipe completion rate (marked as cooked).

---

## 3. Retention Campaign — "The OJAS Streak"

**Objective:** Improve 30-day retention through habit formation, referrals, and light community/UGC mechanics.

**Audience:** Users in week 2–4 of their subscription — the highest-risk churn window.

**Channels:** Push notifications, in-app badges, referral program, private community group (WhatsApp/Discord-style), email.

**Core Message:** "Consistency compounds. So does your streak."

**Sample Copy:**
- *Push notification:* "🔥 12-day streak. One workout stands between you and a new personal best."
- *Referral prompt:* "Bring a training partner. You both get a free month on Pro."

**Content Ideas:** Monthly UGC challenge (e.g. "7-Day Yoga Reset") with a shareable completion badge; streak leaderboards inside the community group; milestone emails at day 7/30/90 with a personalized recap of workouts logged and recipes tried.

**KPIs:** D30 and D90 retention, referral-driven sign-ups, streak completion rate.

---

## 4. Seasonal Campaign — "Off-Season, All Season"

**Objective:** Capture two seasonal demand spikes — New Year resolution traffic (global) and monsoon indoor-fitness search demand (India) — with tailored landing pages and creative.

**Audience:** Seasonal searchers (paid search + social) who aren't already OJAS users.

**Channels:** Paid search, seasonal landing pages, geo-targeted social ads.

**Core Message:**
- *January (global):* "Resolutions fail in week two. Plans that adapt don't."
- *Monsoon (India):* "Rain outside. Routine inside." — indoor HIIT/yoga sequences from the Exercise Library, no gym required.

**Sample Ad Copy:**
- *Headline:* "New year, same excuses? Not this time."
  *Body:* "Start with a 7-day free trial and a daily plan that actually fits your week."
- *Headline (monsoon):* "No gym? No problem."
  *Body:* "20-minute indoor routines from the OJAS Exercise Library — built for small spaces and bad weather."

**Content Ideas:** "New Year, New Plan" quiz-style landing page that routes to a suggested subscription tier; monsoon-themed indoor workout reel series using the existing HIIT/Yoga categories.

**KPIs:** Seasonal cost-per-trial vs. baseline, landing page conversion rate, plan-tier mix of seasonal sign-ups.
